Breadwinners

Research prototype use

Updated October 23, 2026.

Breadwinners CSI POC is an operator-managed research prototype for collecting Wi-Fi measurements, retaining camera evidence, and backing up experiments. Its measurements and charts are exploratory; the app does not certify mold detection or product condition.

The operator controls the devices, recording setup, Google account, collection schedule, and sharing of results. Use the Pi workspace only with authorization from its operator. Recordings and uploads depend on power, network access, available storage, and valid Google authorization; interruptions can delay or prevent backups.

The prototype has no paid subscription or service-level commitment. For support, use the project support email shown on the Google consent screen. See the privacy and Google data-use description for data handling and access controls.
