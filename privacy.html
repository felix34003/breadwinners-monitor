Breadwinners

Privacy and Google data use

Updated October 23, 2026. This describes the Breadwinners CSI POC research uploader and its accompanying Pi workspace.

What the app does

The operator's Raspberry Pi records Wi-Fi channel-state information (CSI), timestamps, device configuration and diagnostics, and configured camera evidence. The uploader backs up these experimental records, charts, databases, and daily archives to the operator's Google Drive.

Google access

The uploader requests the drive.file permission. It creates, reads, updates, and verifies files created by the app or explicitly opened with it, including backup folders, datasets, archives, and delivery metadata. It checks account identity and available Drive storage to operate the backup. It does not request Gmail, Contacts, or unrestricted access to all Drive files.

Use and sharing

Google account information is used to identify the backup account and operate and verify the requested backups. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. The uploader does not automatically make its Drive folders public. The operator can separately choose to share experimental results, charts, or camera evidence; those choices determine their recipients.

Storage and retention

Experimental records are stored on the operator-controlled Pi and in its Google Drive. Local cleanup uses verified-backup checks and configurable retention; pending uploads and protected evidence may remain longer. Cloud records remain until the operator deletes them. OAuth credentials are stored in private configuration on the operator's devices, outside the code repository. This public static website does not contain those credentials or the experimental dataset. GitHub Pages hosts the static website, while the private Pi workspace uses the operator's Tailscale connection and Pi login.

Control and contact

The operator can stop the uploader, remove the stored credential, revoke the app in Google Account third-party access settings, or delete its Drive files. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not delete previously stored records. For questions or deletion requests, contact the project operator using the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
